2. Technical SEO & Canonical Protection Protocol

The 4-stage engineering safeguard to ensure syndication drives high domain authority without duplicate content penalties.

Google-Compliant Syndication Workflow
SEO Protected
PHASE 01: ORIGIN LOCK Host Domain Publishing Article is published first on the client's root domain with full Schema markup (Article, author, datePublished).
PHASE 02: QUARANTINE 14-Day Indexation Hold Syndication is strictly held for 14-21 days until Google Search Console confirms Googlebot has indexed and attributed original authority to the client URL.
PHASE 03: CANONICAL TAG Cross-Domain Canonical Republished copies on Medium, partner blogs, or Substack inject the client URL into the rel="canonical" tag, directing all PageRank to root.
PHASE 04: ATTRIBUTION Editorial Attribution Clear editorial statement at top/bottom: "This article was originally published on [Brand]. Republished with permission." with a direct do-follow backlink.

7. The 16-Point Technical & Syndication Quality Control (QC) Protocol

The operational and SEO checklist applied to every single piece of content before it goes public.

  • ✓
    Aspect Ratio Rigor: 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/TikTok; 4:5 or 1:1 for LinkedIn/IG feeds; 16:9 for YouTube.
  • ✓
    UI Safe Margins: 9:16 text kept away from bottom captions and right-side interactive icons (likes/shares).
  • ✓
    Above-The-Fold Hook Check: First 2-3 lines formatted to hook reader before the "...see more" truncation click.
  • ✓
    External Link Hygiene: LinkedIn external links placed in first comment or featured section to protect algorithm reach.
  • ✓
    Brand Typography & Colors: Strict adherence to brand kit hex codes, font weights, and spacing in Canva.
  • ✓
    Audio Sync & Caption Timing: 100% accurate subtitle timing without text clipping or overlapping words.
  • ✓
    Tagging & Mention Accuracy: Verified spelling of all featured collaborator handles across respective platforms.
  • ✓
    Hashtag Taxonomy: 3-5 curated, high-relevance hashtags rather than generic or algorithmic spam blocks.
  • ✓
    Carousel Pagination: Numbered slides (e.g. 01/07) with directional arrows guiding continuous swipes.
  • ✓
    CTA Alignment: Clear single call-to-action per post (Comment, Save, Share, or Click) avoiding message confusion.
  • ✓
    Asset Tagging & Archival: All final exported files renamed using standard syntax and archived in Notion/Drive.
  • ✓
    Time-Zone Sync: Scheduled to target audience peak activity windows (US Eastern & Central business hours).
  • ✓
    Canonical URL Verification: External syndication URLs verify active rel="canonical" pointing to root origin.
  • ✓
    14-Day Indexation Clearance: Confirmed original post is indexed in Google Cache before external syndication release.
  • ✓
    Editorial Attribution Signature: Standardized disclaimer and do-follow link placed in republished article body.
  • ✓
    UTM Parameter Audit: Verified source, medium, and campaign tokens test successfully in Google Analytics 4.
